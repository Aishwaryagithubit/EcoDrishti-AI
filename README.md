**🌍 EcoDrishti AI**
Building Nepal's Climate Intelligence Infrastructure for Education

"You can't reduce what you can't measure."

EcoDrishti AI is an AI-powered climate intelligence platform designed to help educational institutions quantify, understand, and reduce their environmental impact through data-driven sustainability management.

Unlike traditional carbon calculators that merely estimate emissions, EcoDrishti AI creates a complete climate action ecosystem where schools continuously measure, improve, benchmark, and educate future climate leaders.

The Challenge

Climate change mitigation begins with measurement.

Yet thousands of schools across developing nations operate without access to reliable environmental intelligence.

Current carbon accounting solutions were built for enterprises and governments—not schools.

As a result:

Carbon emissions remain invisible.
Sustainability initiatives lack measurable outcomes.
Environmental education remains theoretical.
Decision-makers operate without climate data.

For Nepal, the challenge is even greater.

Many institutions lack complete operational records, sustainability expertise, dedicated environmental staff, or consistent digital infrastructure.

Without measurement, meaningful climate action becomes impossible.

EcoDrishti AI exists to close this gap.

Why EcoDrishti Matters

Education systems influence future generations more than any other institution.

Every school has:

Energy consumption
Transportation emissions
Waste generation
Water usage
Procurement activities

Yet very few understand their climate footprint.

EcoDrishti transforms schools from passive consumers of environmental resources into active participants in climate stewardship.

The platform turns sustainability from a reporting exercise into a measurable operational discipline.

Product Vision

We believe every school should have access to the same environmental intelligence capabilities available to large corporations.

Our vision is to build:

The operating system for educational sustainability.

A platform where institutions can:

Measure carbon impact

Track long-term sustainability performance
Core Innovation
1. Hybrid Carbon Assessment Framework

Traditional carbon accounting depends on complete datasets.

Schools rarely possess complete datasets.

EcoDrishti introduces a hybrid assessment methodology that combines:

Direct operational inputs
Infrastructure-based estimation models
Proxy indicators
Nepal-specific emission factors
Confidence scoring mechanisms

This enables meaningful sustainability analysis even when environmental data is incomplete.

2. Climate Intelligence Engine

Beyond measurement, EcoDrishti continuously analyses environmental performance to identify:

High-emission activities
Operational inefficiencies
Sustainability opportunities
Reduction potential

The system converts environmental data into actionable intelligence.

3. AI-Powered Action Planning

Most sustainability platforms stop at reporting.

EcoDrishti generates:

School-specific recommendations
Reduction roadmaps
Prioritized interventions
Climate action plans

Each recommendation is evaluated against:

Expected environmental impact
Implementation complexity
Resource requirements
Reduction potential
4. Student-Centered Climate Participation

Climate education becomes significantly more effective when students can observe the consequences of real-world actions.

EcoDrishti integrates:

Climate challenges
Sustainability missions
EcoPoints incentives
Impact tracking

Students become active contributors rather than passive learners.
